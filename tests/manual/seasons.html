Forktown · a study of the seasons

The turning year.

The town keeps one calendar for everyone. Four seasons of 28 town days make a year; a town day lasts 24 real minutes, so a season passes in about 11 real hours. Blossom, fireflies, turning leaves and snow all follow the shared UTC clock, so every visitor sees the same weather at the same moment. Nothing is saved and nothing is random.

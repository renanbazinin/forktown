    05 · The engineer's corner

    Check all mixes

    Renders every record the way the town does and measures it. Flags a peak at or above −1 dBFS, an RMS level below −40 dB and a loop seam of 0.03 or more. Numbers catch clipping and silence; only listening catches the rest.
